Trale ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Trale mobile application.

When you create and use a Trale account, we may collect the following information:

  • Name or username

You should avoid sharing sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information, home addresses, or other information you would not want another user to see.

Trale allows users to send connection requests and communicate through messages. Messages may be stored on our servers so that conversations can be delivered and synchronized between users and devices.

You should remember that messages you send to another user may be viewed, saved, or shared by that user. Trale cannot guarantee that another user will keep your conversations confidential.

  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Investigate reports and enforce our rules.

Trale provides features such as blocking and reporting to help users manage unwanted interactions and report inappropriate behavior.

Information associated with reports, blocks, or suspected abuse may be reviewed and used to investigate violations of our policies, prevent abuse, and protect users.
